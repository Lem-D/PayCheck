Harbour palettes

The rings and tiles layout in fifteen colour palettes. Harbour Gold is first, then four green and red ones that work like a code diff (green is money added, red is money spent), then the gold and warm-metal ones, then the four from the start. Each has a light and a dark version, and the app would switch between them with your phone's setting. In every palette one colour means saving (the ring, goals, the main button) and another means spending.
